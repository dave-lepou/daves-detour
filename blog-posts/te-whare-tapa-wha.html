Te Whare Tapa Whā

In this blog, i'm going to share my wellbeing plan, focussing on the Te Whare Tapa Whā model and the commitment i will make to strengthening each domain!

Te Whare Tapa Whā: My Wellbeing Plan

Taha Tinana (Physical Wellbeing)

  • Go to the gym to do my daily workout
  • Drink a minimum ofv2L of water throughout the day.
  • Aim for 8 hours of sleep each night.

Taha Hinengaro (Mental & Emotional Wellbeing)

  • Journal after study / coding sessions.
  • Practice box-breathing exercises when feeling stuck or tunnel-visioned.

Taha Wairua (Spiritual Wellbeing)

  • Stick to my daily prayer and devotional times - reading and listening to scripture
  • Reflect and "reprogram" each day

Taha Whānau (Family & Social Wellbeing)

  • Spend quality time with my family
  • Actively stay connected to my "village"
  • Refelct with my children daily - checking in on how their day went

Te Whenua (Your Environment)

  • Spending time outdoors
  • Ensuring i am positively influencing different environments i walk in to
  • Investing time and effort upkeeping my own whenua
